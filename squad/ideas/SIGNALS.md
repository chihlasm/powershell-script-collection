# Real-world signals

The single biggest lever on idea quality. The thinker squad weights anything here above
its own reasoning. Drop in raw notes whenever something happens; no formatting needed.

Good things to capture:
- Moments you or a user left the product to use another tool, and what for.
- Tasks or requests that cause the most support load or user frustration.
- Direct feedback from users ("I wish it...", "I never use...").
- Things you do by hand every week that feel automatable.
- Anything a customer asked for that no tool gave them.

Format suggestion: `- YYYY-MM-DD, source: what happened`
