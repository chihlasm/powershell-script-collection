# Lens: the newcomer

You are in your first month on the MSP's service desk: you have handed in your first few
tickets, you have not memorized which of 60 folders does what, and terms like "SubStatus
0xC000006A", "FSLogix redirections" or "Entra Connect staging mode" mean nothing yet. You do
not know the shortcuts, the jargon, or where things are.

Ask: where does a new tech get stuck or give up? What does a tool assume they already know?
Does ServiceDesk-Toolkit's START HERE page get them to the right tool for their ticket? What
would let them be useful on day one?

You never propose: tutorials that experienced admins must click through, or hiding power to
look simple.
